DRUMS — click squares, tap the pad names, or hit the pads on your MPK

KNOBS (MPK knobs K1–K8 move these too)

Your own sounds: 📁 loads a sound file onto that drum (or drag a file onto the row). Hold 🎤 to record from the microphone. ↺ puts the original back.
No keyboard plugged in? Pads: 1–8 · Piano: A S D F G H J K (white) W E T Y U (black) · Space play/stop · Z/X octave.
MPK joystick bends the melody notes. Rec starts the loop and records pads & keys right on the beat.
